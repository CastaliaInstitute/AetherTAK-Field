# AetherTAK Field

## Short description

Secure TAK maps, field observations, sensors, media, and depth capture.

## Full description

AetherTAK Field brings secure TAK collaboration to agriculture and ecological field work.

Use an offline-capable map to see team contacts, crop areas, observations, and sensor readings. Capture geotagged photos, video, notes, field boundaries, and supported LiDAR or AR depth records, then synchronize them with an enrolled AetherTAK server.

Key capabilities include:

- TAK Cursor-on-Target position and GeoChat interoperability
- Secure server enrollment and mutual-TLS transport
- Crop, soil, water, habitat, pest, and ecological observations
- ChirpStack sensor visualization
- Geotagged camera, video, and audio capture
- LiDAR or AR depth capture on supported devices
- Offline field records and queued synchronization
- Encrypted TAK mission-package import and export

AetherTAK Field is intended for authorized teams. A compatible AetherTAK deployment and enrollment package are required for connected collaboration.
