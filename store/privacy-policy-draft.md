# AetherTAK Field privacy notice — draft

**Status: operator and legal review required; do not publish as final.**

AetherTAK Field is an operational field-mapping and collaboration client. It does not contain advertising, third-party advertising SDKs, or cross-app tracking.

The app processes a TAK callsign and UID, location, messages, photos, video, audio, field notes, observations, sensor records, mission-package files, and supported LiDAR or AR depth captures when those features are used. Connected data is sent to the AetherTAK server selected by the user or their organization. Local data supports offline work and queued synchronization.

The organization operating the connected AetherTAK server determines who may access mission data, how long it is retained, and how access, correction, export, and deletion requests are handled. Users should contact that organization's system administrator for those requests.

Camera, microphone, photo-library, local-network, and location access are used only for the corresponding app features. The Android build does not request broad media-library, storage, or background-location access. Data is expected to be protected in transit by HTTPS and TAK mutual TLS; operators must deploy valid endpoints and protect enrollment credentials.

Before publication, replace this paragraph with the legal operator name, postal or electronic contact, effective date, server subprocessors, retention periods, deletion-request process, and jurisdiction-specific notices.
